Bhagavad Gita 10.25

महर्षीणां भृगुरहं गिरामस्म्येकमक्षरम्। यज्ञानां जपयज्ञोऽस्मि स्थावराणां हिमालयः।।10.25।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Among great sages I am Bhrigu; among utterances, the single syllable Om. Among offerings (yajna) I am the offering of recitation; among immovable things, the Himalaya.
