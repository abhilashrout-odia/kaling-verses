Bhagavad Gita 2.35

भयाद्रणादुपरतं मंस्यन्ते त्वां महारथाः। येषां च त्वं बहुमतो भूत्वा यास्यसि लाघवम्।।2.35।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

The great warriors who respect Arjuna will think he left the battle out of fear. Krishna says Arjuna will lose their high regard.
