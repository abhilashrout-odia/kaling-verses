Bhagavad Gita 4.42

तस्मादज्ञानसंभूतं हृत्स्थं ज्ञानासिनाऽऽत्मनः। छित्त्वैनं संशयं योगमातिष्ठोत्तिष्ठ भारत।।4.42।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

So, with the sword of knowledge, cut away this doubt in your heart that was born of ignorance. Take hold of yoga and stand up, Arjuna.
