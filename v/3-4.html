Bhagavad Gita 3.4

न कर्मणामनारम्भान्नैष्कर्म्यं पुरुषोऽश्नुते। न च संन्यसनादेव सिद्धिं समधिगच्छति।।3.4।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

A person does not reach freedom from the binding effect of action just by not starting any action. Nor does he reach perfection simply by giving action up.
