Bhagavad Gita 4.28

द्रव्ययज्ञास्तपोयज्ञा योगयज्ञास्तथापरे। स्वाध्यायज्ञानयज्ञाश्च यतयः संशितव्रताः।।4.28।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Some offer their wealth, others their discipline, others their yoga. Some, firm in their vows, offer study and knowledge.
