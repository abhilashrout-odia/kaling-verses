Bhagavad Gita 9.8

प्रकृतिं स्वामवष्टभ्य विसृजामि पुनः पुनः। भूतग्राममिमं कृत्स्नमवशं प्रकृतेर्वशात्।।9.8।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Presiding over my own nature, I send forth this whole multitude of beings again and again. They are helpless under nature's power.
