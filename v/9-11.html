Bhagavad Gita 9.11

अवजानन्ति मां मूढा मानुषीं तनुमाश्रितम्। परं भावमजानन्तो मम भूतमहेश्वरम्।।9.11।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Fools disregard me when I take a human body, not knowing my higher nature as the great Lord of beings.
