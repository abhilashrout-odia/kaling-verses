Bhagavad Gita 18.29

बुद्धेर्भेदं धृतेश्चैव गुणतस्त्रिविधं श्रृणु।प्रोच्यमानमशेषेण पृथक्त्वेन धनञ्जय।।18.29।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Hear now, Arjuna, the division of understanding (buddhi) and steadiness (dhriti) into three kinds according to the gunas, told fully and one by one.
