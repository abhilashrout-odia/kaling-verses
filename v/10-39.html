Bhagavad Gita 10.39

यच्चापि सर्वभूतानां बीजं तदहमर्जुन। न तदस्ति विना यत्स्यान्मया भूतं चराचरम्।।10.39।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Whatever is the seed of all beings, that am I, Arjuna. No moving or unmoving being can exist without me.
