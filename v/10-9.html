Bhagavad Gita 10.9

मच्चित्ता मद्गतप्राणा बोधयन्तः परस्परम्। कथयन्तश्च मां नित्यं तुष्यन्ति च रमन्ति च।।10.9।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

With minds fixed on me and lives devoted to me, enlightening one another and always speaking of me, they find satisfaction and delight.
