Bhagavad Gita 2.39

एषा तेऽभिहिता सांख्ये बुद्धिर्योगे त्विमां श्रृणु। बुद्ध्यायुक्तो यया पार्थ कर्मबन्धं प्रहास्यसि।।2.39।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Krishna now turns from teaching about the Self to the discipline of action. He tells Arjuna that this understanding can free him from the binding effects of action.
