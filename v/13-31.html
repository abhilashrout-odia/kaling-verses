Bhagavad Gita 13.30

यदा भूतपृथग्भावमेकस्थमनुपश्यति।तत एव च विस्तारं ब्रह्म सम्पद्यते तदा।।13.31।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

When one sees the separate existence of beings as resting in one, and their spreading out from that alone, then one attains Brahman.
