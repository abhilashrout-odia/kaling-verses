Bhagavad Gita 8.15

मामुपेत्य पुनर्जन्म दुःखालयमशाश्वतम्। नाप्नुवन्ति महात्मानः संसिद्धिं परमां गताः।।8.15।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Having reached me, these great souls do not take birth again in this impermanent home of suffering, because they have attained the highest perfection.
