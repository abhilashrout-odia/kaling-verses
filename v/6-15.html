Bhagavad Gita 6.15

युञ्जन्नेवं सदाऽऽत्मानं योगी नियतमानसः। शान्तिं निर्वाणपरमां मत्संस्थामधिगच्छति।।6.15।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

In this way, always keeping the mind steady, the yogi with a controlled mind reaches the peace that ends in liberation and rests in me.
