Bhagavad Gita 11.35

सञ्जय उवाच एतच्छ्रुत्वा वचनं केशवस्य कृताञ्जलिर्वेपमानः किरीटी। नमस्कृत्वा भूय एवाह कृष्णं सगद्गदं भीतभीतः प्रणम्य।।11.35।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Sanjaya said: Hearing Krishna's words, Arjuna trembled with joined hands. Bowing again, overwhelmed with fear, he spoke to Krishna in a faltering voice.
