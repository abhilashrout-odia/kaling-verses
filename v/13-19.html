Bhagavad Gita 13.18

इति क्षेत्रं तथा ज्ञानं ज्ञेयं चोक्तं समासतः।मद्भक्त एतद्विज्ञाय मद्भावायोपपद्यते।।13.19।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Thus the field, knowledge and the object of knowledge have been told in brief. My devotee, knowing this, becomes fit for my state of being.
