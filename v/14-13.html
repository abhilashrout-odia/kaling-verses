Bhagavad Gita 14.13

अप्रकाशोऽप्रवृत्तिश्च प्रमादो मोह एव च।तमस्येतानि जायन्ते विवृद्धे कुरुनन्दन।।14.13।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Darkness, inactivity, carelessness and delusion - these arise when tamas increases, joy of the Kurus.
