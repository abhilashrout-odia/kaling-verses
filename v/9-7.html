Bhagavad Gita 9.7

सर्वभूतानि कौन्तेय प्रकृतिं यान्ति मामिकाम्। कल्पक्षये पुनस्तानि कल्पादौ विसृजाम्यहम्।।9.7।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

At the end of a cosmic cycle, Arjuna, all beings enter my nature. At the beginning of a cycle, I send them forth again.
