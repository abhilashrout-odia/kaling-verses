Bhagavad Gita 9.13

महात्मानस्तु मां पार्थ दैवीं प्रकृतिमाश्रिताः। भजन्त्यनन्यमनसो ज्ञात्वा भूतादिमव्ययम्।।9.13।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

But great souls, Arjuna, take refuge in divine nature. Knowing me as the imperishable source of beings, they worship me with an undivided mind.
