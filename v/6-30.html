Bhagavad Gita 6.30

यो मां पश्यति सर्वत्र सर्वं च मयि पश्यति। तस्याहं न प्रणश्यामि स च मे न प्रणश्यति।।6.30।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

One who sees me everywhere and sees everything in me is never lost to me, and I am never lost to that person.
