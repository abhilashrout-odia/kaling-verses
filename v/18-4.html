Bhagavad Gita 18.4

निश्चयं श्रृणु मे तत्र त्यागे भरतसत्तम।त्यागो हि पुरुषव्याघ्र त्रिविधः संप्रकीर्तितः।।18.4।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Hear my conclusion about giving up, best of the Bharatas; giving up, tiger among men, has been declared to be of three kinds.
