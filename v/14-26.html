Bhagavad Gita 14.26

मां च योऽव्यभिचारेण भक्तियोगेन सेवते।स गुणान्समतीत्यैतान् ब्रह्मभूयाय कल्पते।।14.26।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

One who serves me with unwavering devotion (bhakti yoga) goes beyond these gunas and becomes fit for oneness with Brahman.
