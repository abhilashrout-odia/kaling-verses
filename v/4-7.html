Bhagavad Gita 4.7

यदा यदा हि धर्मस्य ग्लानिर्भवति भारत। अभ्युत्थानमधर्मस्य तदाऽऽत्मानं सृजाम्यहम्।।4.7।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Krishna tells Arjuna that whenever dharma declines and adharma gains strength, he brings himself into manifestation.
