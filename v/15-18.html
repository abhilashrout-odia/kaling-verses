Bhagavad Gita 15.18

यस्मात्क्षरमतीतोऽहमक्षरादपि चोत्तमः।अतोऽस्मि लोके वेदे च प्रथितः पुरुषोत्तमः।।15.18।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Since I am beyond the perishable and even higher than the imperishable, I am known in the world and in the Veda as the supreme person (Purushottama).

Hindi (AI drafted, not reviewed)
