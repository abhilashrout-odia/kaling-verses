Bhagavad Gita 2.61

तानि सर्वाणि संयम्य युक्त आसीत मत्परः। वशे हि यस्येन्द्रियाणि तस्य प्रज्ञा प्रतिष्ठिता।।2.61।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Krishna asks Arjuna to restrain the senses and remain focused on him as the supreme goal. A person whose senses are under control has steady wisdom.
