Bhagavad Gita 12.7

तेषामहं समुद्धर्ता मृत्युसंसारसागरात्।भवामि नचिरात्पार्थ मय्यावेशितचेतसाम्।।12.7।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

For them, whose minds are set on me, Arjuna, I become before long the one who lifts them out of the ocean of death and worldly cycle (samsara).
