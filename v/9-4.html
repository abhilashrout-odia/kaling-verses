Bhagavad Gita 9.4

मया ततमिदं सर्वं जगदव्यक्तमूर्तिना। मत्स्थानि सर्वभूतानि न चाहं तेष्ववस्थितः।।9.4।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

By my unmanifest form I pervade this entire world. All beings abide in me, but I do not abide in them.
