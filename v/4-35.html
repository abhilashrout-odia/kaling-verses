Bhagavad Gita 4.35

यज्ज्ञात्वा न पुनर्मोहमेवं यास्यसि पाण्डव। येन भूतान्यशेषेण द्रक्ष्यस्यात्मन्यथो मयि।।4.35।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Once you know this, you will not fall into confusion again. Through it you will see all beings within yourself, and then in me.
