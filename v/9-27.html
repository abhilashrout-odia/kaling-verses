Bhagavad Gita 9.27

यत्करोषि यदश्नासि यज्जुहोषि ददासि यत्। यत्तपस्यसि कौन्तेय तत्कुरुष्व मदर्पणम्।।9.27।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Whatever you do, eat, offer into the fire, give or practice as austerity, Arjuna, do it as an offering to me.
