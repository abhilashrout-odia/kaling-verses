Bhagavad Gita 18.58

मच्चित्तः सर्वदुर्गाणि मत्प्रसादात्तरिष्यसि।अथ चेत्त्वमहङ्कारान्न श्रोष्यसि विनङ्क्ष्यसि।।18.58।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

With your mind on me, you will cross over all difficulties by my grace; but if out of egoism you do not listen, you will be lost.
