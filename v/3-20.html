Bhagavad Gita 3.20

कर्मणैव हि संसिद्धिमास्थिता जनकादयः। लोकसंग्रहमेवापि संपश्यन्कर्तुमर्हसि।।3.20।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Janaka and others reached perfection through action itself. Keeping in view the good of the world, you too should act.
