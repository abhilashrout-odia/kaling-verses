Bhagavad Gita 6.23

तं विद्याद् दुःखसंयोगवियोगं योगसंज्ञितम्। स निश्चयेन योक्तव्यो योगोऽनिर्विण्णचेतसा।।6.23।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Know that this, the cutting off of the union with suffering, is called yoga. It should be practised with determination and with a mind that does not lose heart.
