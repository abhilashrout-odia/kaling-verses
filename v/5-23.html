Bhagavad Gita 5.23

शक्नोतीहैव यः सोढुं प्राक्शरीरविमोक्षणात्। कामक्रोधोद्भवं वेगं स युक्तः स सुखी नरः।।5.23।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

One who can withstand, even before leaving the body, the surge of desire and anger is a yogi and a happy person.
