Bhagavad Gita 8.13

ओमित्येकाक्षरं ब्रह्म व्याहरन्मामनुस्मरन्। यः प्रयाति त्यजन्देहं स याति परमां गतिम्।।8.13।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Uttering the single syllable Om, which is Brahman, and remembering me, one who leaves the body in this way reaches the supreme goal.
