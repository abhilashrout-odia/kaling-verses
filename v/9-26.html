Bhagavad Gita 9.26

पत्रं पुष्पं फलं तोयं यो मे भक्त्या प्रयच्छति। तदहं भक्त्युपहृतमश्नामि प्रयतात्मनः।।9.26।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Whoever offers me a leaf, a flower, a fruit or water with devotion, I accept that devoted offering from one whose self is pure.
