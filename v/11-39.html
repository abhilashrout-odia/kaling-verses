Bhagavad Gita 11.39

वायुर्यमोऽग्निर्वरुणः शशाङ्कः प्रजापतिस्त्वं प्रपितामहश्च। नमो नमस्तेऽस्तु सहस्रकृत्वः पुनश्च भूयोऽपि नमो नमस्ते।।11.39।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

You are Vayu, Yama, Agni, Varuna, the moon, Prajapati and the great-grandfather. Salutations to you a thousand times, and again and again, salutations to you.
