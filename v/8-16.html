Bhagavad Gita 8.16

आब्रह्मभुवनाल्लोकाः पुनरावर्तिनोऽर्जुन। मामुपेत्य तु कौन्तेय पुनर्जन्म न विद्यते।।8.16।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

All worlds, up to the realm of Brahma, come round again, Arjuna. But for one who reaches me, there is no rebirth.
