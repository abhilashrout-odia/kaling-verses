Bhagavad Gita 18.71

श्रद्धावाननसूयश्च श्रृणुयादपि यो नरः।सोऽपि मुक्तः शुभाँल्लोकान्प्राप्नुयात्पुण्यकर्मणाम्।।18.71।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Even a person who merely listens with faith and without fault-finding, being liberated, will attain the auspicious worlds of those whose deeds are virtuous.
