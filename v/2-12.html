Bhagavad Gita 2.12

न त्वेवाहं जातु नासं न त्वं नेमे जनाधिपाः। न चैव न भविष्यामः सर्वे वयमतः परम्।।2.12।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Krishna says that he, Arjuna and the gathered kings did not begin with this moment, and their existence will not simply end afterward. The teaching points beyond the body to enduring being.

Hindi (AI drafted, not reviewed)
