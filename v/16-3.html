Bhagavad Gita 16.3

तेजः क्षमा धृतिः शौचमद्रोहो नातिमानिता। भवन्ति सम्पदं दैवीमभिजातस्य भारत।।16.3।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Vigour, forgiveness, fortitude, purity, freedom from malice and from excessive pride - these belong, Arjuna, to one born with the divine endowment.
