Bhagavad Gita 1.34

आचार्याः पितरः पुत्रास्तथैव च पितामहाः। मातुलाः श्चशुराः पौत्राः श्यालाः सम्बन्धिनस्तथा।।1.34।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Those for whom we wanted a kingdom, pleasures and happiness (from the previous verse) are these: teachers, fathers, sons, grandfathers, maternal uncles, fathers-in-law, grandsons, brothers-in-law and other relatives.
