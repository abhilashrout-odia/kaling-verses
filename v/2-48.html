Bhagavad Gita 2.48

योगस्थः कुरु कर्माणि सङ्गं त्यक्त्वा धनञ्जय। सिद्ध्यसिद्ध्योः समो भूत्वा समत्वं योग उच्यते॥

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Do your work with a steady mind, without clinging to the result. Stay balanced in success and failure. This balance is called yoga.
