Bhagavad Gita 11.17

किरीटिनं गदिनं चक्रिणं च तेजोराशिं सर्वतोदीप्तिमन्तम्। पश्यामि त्वां दुर्निरीक्ष्यं समन्ता द्दीप्तानलार्कद्युतिमप्रमेयम्।।11.17।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

I see you crowned, bearing mace and discus, a mass of radiance shining everywhere, immeasurable and hard to look upon, with the brightness of blazing fire and sun.
