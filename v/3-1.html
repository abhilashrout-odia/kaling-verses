Bhagavad Gita 3.1

अर्जुन उवाच ज्यायसी चेत्कर्मणस्ते मता बुद्धिर्जनार्दन। तत्किं कर्मणि घोरे मां नियोजयसि केशव।।3.1।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Arjuna said: If you think understanding is higher than action, Krishna, why do you urge me to this terrible work?
