Bhagavad Gita 1.1

धृतराष्ट्र उवाच धर्मक्षेत्रे कुरुक्षेत्रे समवेता युयुत्सवः। मामकाः पाण्डवाश्चैव किमकुर्वत सञ्जय।।1.1।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Dhritarashtra asked: On the field of Kurukshetra, the land of dharma, my sons and the sons of Pandu have gathered, eager to fight. Sanjaya, what did they do?
