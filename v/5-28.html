Bhagavad Gita 5.28

यतेन्द्रियमनोबुद्धिर्मुनिर्मोक्षपरायणः। विगतेच्छाभयक्रोधो यः सदा मुक्त एव सः।।5.28।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

the sage whose senses, mind and intellect are in hand, who is set on freedom, and free of desire, fear and anger, is always free.
