Bhagavad Gita 1.18

द्रुपदो द्रौपदेयाश्च सर्वशः पृथिवीपते। सौभद्रश्च महाबाहुः शङ्खान्दध्मुः पृथक्पृथक्।।1.18।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Drupada, the sons of Draupadi, and the mighty-armed son of Subhadra, O lord of the earth, each blew their own conch.
