Bhagavad Gita 8.4

अधिभूतं क्षरो भावः पुरुषश्चाधिदैवतम्। अधियज्ञोऽहमेवात्र देहे देहभृतां वर।।8.4।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

The perishable nature is the realm of beings (adhibhuta), the cosmic person is the realm of the divine (adhidaiva), and I myself, here in this body, am adhiyajna, the one to whom offerings are made.
