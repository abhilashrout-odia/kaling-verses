Bhagavad Gita 9.9

न च मां तानि कर्माणि निबध्नन्ति धनञ्जय। उदासीनवदासीनमसक्तं तेषु कर्मसु।।9.9।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Those actions do not bind me, Arjuna. I remain as one uninvolved, unattached to those actions.
