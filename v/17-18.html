Bhagavad Gita 17.18

सत्कारमानपूजार्थं तपो दम्भेन चैव यत्।क्रियते तदिह प्रोक्तं राजसं चलमध्रुवम्।।17.18।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Austerity performed for the sake of respect, honour and worship, and with hypocrisy, is said here to be rajasic, unstable and impermanent.
