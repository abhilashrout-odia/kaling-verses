Bhagavad Gita 15.13

गामाविश्य च भूतानि धारयाम्यहमोजसा।पुष्णामि चौषधीः सर्वाः सोमो भूत्वा रसात्मकः।।15.13।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Entering the earth, I support beings with my energy; becoming the moon (soma), full of sap, I nourish all plants.
