Bhagavad Gita 13.16

अविभक्तं च भूतेषु विभक्तमिव च स्थितम्। भूतभर्तृ च तज्ज्ञेयं ग्रसिष्णु प्रभविष्णु च॥

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

The divine is one, though it seems separate in different beings. It sustains all life, brings life into being and receives it when it ends.
