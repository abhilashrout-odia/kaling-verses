Bhagavad Gita 3.27

प्रकृतेः क्रियमाणानि गुणैः कर्माणि सर्वशः। अहङ्कारविमूढात्मा कर्ताऽहमिति मन्यते।।3.27।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

All actions are carried out by the qualities of nature. But a mind confused by ego thinks, "I am the doer."
