Bhagavad Gita 18.64

सर्वगुह्यतमं भूयः श्रृणु मे परमं वचः।इष्टोऽसि मे दृढमिति ततो वक्ष्यामि ते हितम्।।18.64।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Hear again my highest word, the most secret of all. You are deeply dear to me, so I shall speak what is for your good.
