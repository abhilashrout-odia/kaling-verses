Bhagavad Gita 5.29

भोक्तारं यज्ञतपसां सर्वलोकमहेश्वरम्। सुहृदं सर्वभूतानां ज्ञात्वा मां शान्तिमृच्छति।।5.29।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Knowing me as the one who receives offerings and austerities, the great lord of all the worlds and the friend of all beings, a person finds peace.
