Bhagavad Gita 7.13

त्रिभिर्गुणमयैर्भावैरेभिः सर्वमिदं जगत्। मोहितं नाभिजानाति मामेभ्यः परमव्ययम्।।7.13।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Confused by these three kinds of states, the whole world does not know me, who am beyond them and unchanging.
