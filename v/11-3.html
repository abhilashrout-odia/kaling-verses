Bhagavad Gita 11.3

एवमेतद्यथात्थ त्वमात्मानं परमेश्वर। द्रष्टुमिच्छामि ते रूपमैश्वरं पुरुषोत्तम।।11.3।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Supreme Lord, you are as you have described yourself. Supreme Person, I wish to see your divine form.
