Bhagavad Gita 1.30

गाण्डीवं स्रंसते हस्तात्त्वक्चैव परिदह्यते। न च शक्नोम्यवस्थातुं भ्रमतीव च मे मनः।।1.30।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

The bow Gandiva slips from his hand and his skin burns. He cannot stay steady, and his mind feels as if it is spinning.
