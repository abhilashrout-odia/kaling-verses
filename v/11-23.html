Bhagavad Gita 11.23

रूपं महत्ते बहुवक्त्रनेत्रं महाबाहो बहुबाहूरुपादम्। बहूदरं बहुदंष्ट्राकरालं दृष्ट्वा लोकाः प्रव्यथितास्तथाऽहम्।।11.23।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Mighty-armed one, seeing your vast form with many mouths, eyes, arms, thighs, feet and bellies, terrifying with many tusks, the worlds tremble, and so do I.
