Bhagavad Gita 10.23

रुद्राणां शङ्करश्चास्मि वित्तेशो यक्षरक्षसाम्। वसूनां पावकश्चास्मि मेरुः शिखरिणामहम्।।10.23।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Among Rudras I am Shankara; among Yakshas and Rakshasas, the lord of wealth. Among Vasus I am fire; among mountains, Meru.
