Bhagavad Gita 1.6

युधामन्युश्च विक्रान्त उत्तमौजाश्च वीर्यवान्। सौभद्रो द्रौपदेयाश्च सर्व एव महारथाः।।1.6।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Yudhamanyu the bold, Uttamauja the brave, the son of Subhadra, and the sons of Draupadi: all of them are great chariot-warriors.
