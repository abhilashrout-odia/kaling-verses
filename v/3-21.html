Bhagavad Gita 3.21

यद्यदाचरति श्रेष्ठस्तत्तदेवेतरो जनः। स यत्प्रमाणं कुरुते लोकस्तदनुवर्तते॥

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

People follow the example of someone they look up to. The standard that person sets becomes a guide for others.
