Bhagavad Gita 2.44

भोगैश्वर्यप्रसक्तानां तयापहृतचेतसाम्। व्यवसायात्मिका बुद्धिः समाधौ न विधीयते।।2.44।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Those attached to pleasure and power can be carried away by these promises. Krishna says that a steady, focused understanding for meditation does not take root in a mind drawn away like this.
