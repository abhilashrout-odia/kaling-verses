Bhagavad Gita 8.28

वेदेषु यज्ञेषु तपःसु चैव दानेषु यत्पुण्यफलं प्रदिष्टम्। अत्येति तत्सर्वमिदं विदित्वा योगी परं स्थानमुपैति चाद्यम्।।8.28।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

A yogi who knows this goes beyond the results of studying the Vedas, of offerings, of austerities and of gifts, and reaches the supreme original place.
