Bhagavad Gita 12.5

क्लेशोऽधिकतरस्तेषामव्यक्तासक्तचेतसाम्। अव्यक्ता हि गतिर्दुःखं देहवद्भिरवाप्यते।।12.5।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

The struggle is greater for those whose minds are fixed on the unmanifest. For embodied beings, the path of the unmanifest is reached only with difficulty.
