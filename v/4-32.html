Bhagavad Gita 4.32

एवं बहुविधा यज्ञा वितता ब्रह्मणो मुखे। कर्मजान्विद्धि तान्सर्वानेवं ज्ञात्वा विमोक्ष्यसे।।4.32।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Many kinds of offering (yajna) are spread out in the mouth of the sacred word. Know that all of them are born of action, and knowing this you will be freed.
