Bhagavad Gita 2.24

अच्छेद्योऽयमदाह्योऽयमक्लेद्योऽशोष्य एव च। नित्यः सर्वगतः स्थाणुरचलोऽयं सनातनः।।2.24।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

The Self cannot be split, burned, soaked or dried. Krishna describes it as enduring, present everywhere, steady, unmoving and without beginning.
