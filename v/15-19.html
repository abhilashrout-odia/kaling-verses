Bhagavad Gita 15.19

यो मामेवमसम्मूढो जानाति पुरुषोत्तमम्।स सर्वविद्भजति मां सर्वभावेन भारत।।15.19।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

One who, free of delusion, knows me thus as the supreme person, knows everything and worships me with his whole being, Arjuna.
