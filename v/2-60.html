Bhagavad Gita 2.60

यततो ह्यपि कौन्तेय पुरुषस्य विपश्चितः। इन्द्रियाणि प्रमाथीनि हरन्ति प्रसभं मनः।।2.60।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Krishna warns Arjuna that the turbulent senses can forcefully carry away the mind, even when a discerning person is trying to restrain them.
