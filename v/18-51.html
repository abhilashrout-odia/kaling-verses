Bhagavad Gita 18.51

बुद्ध्या विशुद्धया युक्तो धृत्याऽऽत्मानं नियम्य च।शब्दादीन् विषयांस्त्यक्त्वा रागद्वेषौ व्युदस्य च।।18.51।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Endowed with a pure understanding, restraining oneself with steadiness, giving up sound and the other sense objects, and setting aside attraction and aversion,
