Bhagavad Gita 6.45

प्रयत्नाद्यतमानस्तु योगी संशुद्धकिल्बिषः। अनेकजन्मसंसिद्धस्ततो याति परां गतिम्।।6.45।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Striving with effort, cleansed of wrong, and perfected over many births, the yogi then reaches the highest goal.
