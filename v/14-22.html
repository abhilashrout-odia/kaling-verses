Bhagavad Gita 14.22

श्री भगवानुवाचप्रकाशं च प्रवृत्तिं च मोहमेव च पाण्डव।न द्वेष्टि सम्प्रवृत्तानि न निवृत्तानि काङ्क्षति।।14.22।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

The Blessed Lord said: One who does not hate light, activity or delusion when they are present, nor longs for them when they are gone, Arjuna,
