Bhagavad Gita 11.21

अमी हि त्वां सुरसङ्घाः विशन्ति केचिद्भीताः प्राञ्जलयो गृणन्ति। स्वस्तीत्युक्त्वा महर्षिसिद्धसङ्घाः स्तुवन्ति त्वां स्तुतिभिः पुष्कलाभिः।।11.21।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

These hosts of gods enter you. Some, afraid, praise you with joined hands. Hosts of great sages and perfected beings say, 'May there be well-being,' and praise you with abundant hymns.
