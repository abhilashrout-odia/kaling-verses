Bhagavad Gita 4.39

श्रद्धावाँल्लभते ज्ञानं तत्परः संयतेन्द्रियः। ज्ञानं लब्ध्वा परां शान्तिमचिरेणाधिगच्छति।।4.39।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

A person with faith, who is devoted and has the senses in hand, gains knowledge. Having gained it, they quickly reach lasting peace.
