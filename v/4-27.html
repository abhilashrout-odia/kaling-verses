Bhagavad Gita 4.27

सर्वाणीन्द्रियकर्माणि प्राणकर्माणि चापरे। आत्मसंयमयोगाग्नौ जुह्वति ज्ञानदीपिते।।4.27।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Others offer all the work of the senses and of the life-breath into the fire of self-mastery, lit by knowledge.
