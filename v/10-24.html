Bhagavad Gita 10.24

पुरोधसां च मुख्यं मां विद्धि पार्थ बृहस्पतिम्। सेनानीनामहं स्कन्दः सरसामस्मि सागरः।।10.24।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Among priests, Arjuna, know me as their chief, Brihaspati. Among commanders I am Skanda; among bodies of water, the ocean.
