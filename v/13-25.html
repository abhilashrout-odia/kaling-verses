Bhagavad Gita 13.24

ध्यानेनात्मनि पश्यन्ति केचिदात्मानमात्मना।अन्ये सांख्येन योगेन कर्मयोगेन चापरे।।13.25।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Some see the self within themselves, by themselves, through meditation; others through the path of knowledge (Sankhya yoga); and others through the yoga of action (karma yoga).
