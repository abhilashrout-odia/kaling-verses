Bhagavad Gita 18.6

एतान्यपि तु कर्माणि सङ्गं त्यक्त्वा फलानि च।कर्तव्यानीति मे पार्थ निश्चितं मतमुत्तमम्।।18.6।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

But even these actions should be done, giving up attachment and their rewards. This, Arjuna, is my firm and best view.
