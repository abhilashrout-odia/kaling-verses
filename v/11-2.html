Bhagavad Gita 11.2

भवाप्ययौ हि भूतानां श्रुतौ विस्तरशो मया। त्वत्तः कमलपत्राक्ष माहात्म्यमपि चाव्ययम्।।11.2।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Lotus-eyed Krishna, I have heard from you in detail of the arising and passing of beings, and of your imperishable greatness.
