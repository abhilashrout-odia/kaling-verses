Bhagavad Gita 16.22

एतैर्विमुक्तः कौन्तेय तमोद्वारैस्त्रिभिर्नरः।आचरत्यात्मनः श्रेयस्ततो याति परां गतिम्।।16.22।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

One who is freed from these three gates of darkness, Arjuna, practises what is good for the self, and then goes to the highest goal.
