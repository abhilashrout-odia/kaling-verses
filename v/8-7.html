Bhagavad Gita 8.7

तस्मात्सर्वेषु कालेषु मामनुस्मर युध्य च। मय्यर्पितमनोबुद्धिर्मामेवैष्यस्यसंशयम्।।8.7।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

So remember me at all times, and fight. With your mind and intellect offered to me, you will come to me without doubt.
