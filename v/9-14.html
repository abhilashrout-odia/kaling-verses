Bhagavad Gita 9.14

सततं कीर्तयन्तो मां यतन्तश्च दृढव्रताः। नमस्यन्तश्च मां भक्त्या नित्ययुक्ता उपासते।।9.14।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Always praising me, striving with firm vows, and bowing to me in devotion, they worship me, constantly united with me.
