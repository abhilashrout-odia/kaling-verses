Bhagavad Gita 10.40

नान्तोऽस्ति मम दिव्यानां विभूतीनां परंतप। एष तूद्देशतः प्रोक्तो विभूतेर्विस्तरो मया।।10.40।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

There is no end to my divine manifestations, Arjuna. I have described this extent of them only by a few indications.
