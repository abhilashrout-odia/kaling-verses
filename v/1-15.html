Bhagavad Gita 1.15

पाञ्चजन्यं हृषीकेशो देवदत्तं धनंजयः। पौण्ड्रं दध्मौ महाशङ्खं भीमकर्मा वृकोदरः।।1.15।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Krishna blew Panchajanya, Arjuna blew Devadatta, and Bhima, whose deeds are fearsome, blew the great conch Paundra.
