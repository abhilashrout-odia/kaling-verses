Bhagavad Gita 11.41

सखेति मत्वा प्रसभं यदुक्तं हे कृष्ण हे यादव हे सखेति। अजानता महिमानं तवेदं मया प्रमादात्प्रणयेन वापि।।11.41।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Thinking of you as a friend, whatever I rashly said, 'Krishna, Yadava, my friend,' not knowing this greatness of yours, through carelessness or affection,
