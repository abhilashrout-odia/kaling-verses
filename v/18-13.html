Bhagavad Gita 18.13

पञ्चैतानि महाबाहो कारणानि निबोध मे।सांख्ये कृतान्ते प्रोक्तानि सिद्धये सर्वकर्मणाम्।।18.13।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Learn from me, mighty-armed, these five causes, stated in the Sankhya teaching, for the accomplishment of all actions.
