Bhagavad Gita 2.41

व्यवसायात्मिका बुद्धिरेकेह कुरुनन्दन। बहुशाखा ह्यनन्ताश्च बुद्धयोऽव्यवसायिनाम्।।2.41।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

On this path, a clear and steady understanding gives direction. When that resolve is missing, the mind follows countless competing aims.
