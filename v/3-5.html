Bhagavad Gita 3.5

न हि कश्चित्क्षणमपि जातु तिष्ठत्यकर्मकृत्। कार्यते ह्यवशः कर्म सर्वः प्रकृतिजैर्गुणैः।।3.5।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

No one can stay without acting even for a moment. Everyone is driven to act by the pulls of their own nature.
