Bhagavad Gita 17.13

विधिहीनमसृष्टान्नं मन्त्रहीनमदक्षिणम्।श्रद्धाविरहितं यज्ञं तामसं परिचक्षते।।17.13।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

An offering (yajna) without rule, without giving of food, without sacred words, without gifts to the priests, and without faith is called tamasic.
