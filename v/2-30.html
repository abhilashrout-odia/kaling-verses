Bhagavad Gita 2.30

देही नित्यमवध्योऽयं देहे सर्वस्य भारत। तस्मात्सर्वाणि भूतानि न त्वं शोचितुमर्हसि।।2.30।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Krishna says the Self dwelling in every body can never be killed. On that basis, he tells Arjuna not to grieve for any being.
