Bhagavad Gita 5.21

बाह्यस्पर्शेष्वसक्तात्मा विन्दत्यात्मनि यत्सुखम्। स ब्रह्मयोगयुक्तात्मा सुखमक्षयमश्नुते।।5.21।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Not attached to outer contacts, one finds happiness in the self. Joined with Brahman through yoga, one reaches happiness that does not fade.
