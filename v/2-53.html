Bhagavad Gita 2.53

श्रुतिविप्रतिपन्ना ते यदा स्थास्यति निश्चला। समाधावचला बुद्धिस्तदा योगमवाप्स्यसि।।2.53।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

When an understanding unsettled by conflicting teachings becomes steady in contemplation of the Self, Krishna says Arjuna will attain yoga.
