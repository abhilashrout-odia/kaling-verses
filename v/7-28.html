Bhagavad Gita 7.28

येषां त्वन्तगतं पापं जनानां पुण्यकर्मणाम्। ते द्वन्द्वमोहनिर्मुक्ता भजन्ते मां दृढव्रताः।।7.28।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

But those of virtuous action, whose wrong has come to an end, freed from the confusion of opposites, worship me with firm resolve.
