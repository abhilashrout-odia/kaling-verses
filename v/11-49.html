Bhagavad Gita 11.49

मा ते व्यथा मा च विमूढभावो दृष्ट्वा रूपं घोरमीदृङ्ममेदम्। व्यपेतभीः प्रीतमनाः पुनस्त्वं तदेव मे रूपमिदं प्रपश्य।।11.49।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Do not be distressed or bewildered at seeing this terrible form of mine. Free from fear and with a glad mind, behold again that former form of mine.
