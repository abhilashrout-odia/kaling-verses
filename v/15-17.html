Bhagavad Gita 15.17

उत्तमः पुरुषस्त्वन्यः परमात्मेत्युदाहृतः।यो लोकत्रयमाविश्य बिभर्त्यव्यय ईश्वरः।।15.17।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

But there is another, the highest person, called the supreme self (paramatma), the imperishable lord who enters the three worlds and sustains them.
