Bhagavad Gita 10.19

श्री भगवानुवाच हन्त ते कथयिष्यामि दिव्या ह्यात्मविभूतयः। प्राधान्यतः कुरुश्रेष्ठ नास्त्यन्तो विस्तरस्य मे।।10.19।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

The Lord said: Yes, I shall tell you my chief divine manifestations, best of the Kurus, for there is no end to their extent.
