Bhagavad Gita 2.17

अविनाशि तु तद्विद्धि येन सर्वमिदं ततम्। विनाशमव्ययस्यास्य न कश्चित् कर्तुमर्हति।।2.17।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Krishna points to an undying reality that fills all existence. Nothing can bring an end to this reality, which does not wear away.
