Bhagavad Gita 7.3

मनुष्याणां सहस्रेषु कश्चिद्यतति सिद्धये। यततामपि सिद्धानां कश्चिन्मां वेत्ति तत्त्वतः।।7.3।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Among thousands of people, hardly one strives for perfection, and among those who strive and succeed, hardly one knows me in truth.
