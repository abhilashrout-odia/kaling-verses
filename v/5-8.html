Bhagavad Gita 5.8

नैव किंचित्करोमीति युक्तो मन्येत तत्त्ववित्। पश्यन् श्रृणवन्स्पृशञ्जिघ्रन्नश्नन्गच्छन्स्वपन् श्वसन्।।5.8।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

A knower of the truth, absorbed in yoga, thinks: 'I am not doing anything at all', while seeing, hearing, touching, smelling, eating, going, sleeping and breathing,
