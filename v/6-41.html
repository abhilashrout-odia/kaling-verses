Bhagavad Gita 6.41

प्राप्य पुण्यकृतां लोकानुषित्वा शाश्वतीः समाः। शुचीनां श्रीमतां गेहे योगभ्रष्टोऽभिजायते।।6.41।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Having reached the worlds of the good and lived there for many years, the one who slipped from yoga is born in the home of the pure and prosperous.
