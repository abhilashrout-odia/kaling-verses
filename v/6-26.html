Bhagavad Gita 6.26

यतो यतो निश्चरति मनश्चञ्चलमस्थिरम्। ततस्ततो नियम्यैतदात्मन्येव वशं नयेत्।।6.26।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Wherever the restless, unsteady mind wanders, from each of those places let him restrain it and bring it under the control of the self alone.
