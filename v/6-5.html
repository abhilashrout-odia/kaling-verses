Bhagavad Gita 6.5

उद्धरेदात्मनात्मानं नात्मानमवसादयेत्। आत्मैव ह्यात्मनो बन्धुरात्मैव रिपुरात्मनः॥

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Use your mind to lift yourself up, not pull yourself down. Your mind can be your friend, or it can become your enemy.
