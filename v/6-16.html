Bhagavad Gita 6.16

नात्यश्नतस्तु योगोऽस्ति न चैकान्तमनश्नतः। न चातिस्वप्नशीलस्य जाग्रतो नैव चार्जुन।।6.16।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Yoga is not for one who eats too much or who does not eat at all, nor for one who sleeps too much or hardly sleeps, Arjuna.

Hindi (AI drafted, not reviewed)
