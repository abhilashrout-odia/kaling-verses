Bhagavad Gita 8.5

अन्तकाले च मामेव स्मरन्मुक्त्वा कलेवरम्। यः प्रयाति स मद्भावं याति नास्त्यत्र संशयः।।8.5।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

One who, at the end of life, leaves the body remembering me alone, comes to my state. There is no doubt about this.
