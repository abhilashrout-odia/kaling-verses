Bhagavad Gita 18.36

सुखं त्विदानीं त्रिविधं श्रृणु मे भरतर्षभ।अभ्यासाद्रमते यत्र दुःखान्तं च निगच्छति।।18.36।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Now hear from me, best of the Bharatas, the three kinds of happiness - that in which one comes to rejoice through practice, and reaches the end of sorrow;
