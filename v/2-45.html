Bhagavad Gita 2.45

त्रैगुण्यविषया वेदा निस्त्रैगुण्यो भवार्जुन। निर्द्वन्द्वो नित्यसत्त्वस्थो निर्योगक्षेम आत्मवान्।।2.45।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

The Vedic ritual teachings concern the three qualities of nature. Krishna asks Arjuna to rise beyond them and the pairs of opposites, remain in clarity and goodness, let go of anxious acquiring and keeping, and be rooted in the Self.
