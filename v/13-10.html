Bhagavad Gita 13.9

असक्तिरनभिष्वङ्गः पुत्रदारगृहादिषु।नित्यं च समचित्तत्वमिष्टानिष्टोपपत्तिषु।।13.10।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Non-attachment, not clinging to son, wife, home and the like, and constant evenness of mind in the arrival of the wanted and the unwanted,
