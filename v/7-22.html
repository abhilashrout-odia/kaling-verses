Bhagavad Gita 7.22

स तया श्रद्धया युक्तस्तस्याराधनमीहते। लभते च ततः कामान्मयैव विहितान् हि तान्।।7.22।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Joined with that faith, he seeks to worship that form and gets from it the desires he wished for, which in truth were granted by me alone.
