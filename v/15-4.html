Bhagavad Gita 15.4

ततः पदं तत्परिमार्गितव्य यस्मिन्गता न निवर्तन्ति भूयः।तमेव चाद्यं पुरुषं प्रपद्ये यतः प्रवृत्तिः प्रसृता पुराणी।।15.4।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

then that state is to be sought, from which those who go do not return again; I take refuge in that primal person from whom this ancient activity has spread.
