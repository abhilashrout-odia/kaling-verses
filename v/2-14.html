Bhagavad Gita 2.14

मात्रास्पर्शास्तु कौन्तेय शीतोष्णसुखदुःखदाः। आगमापायिनोऽनित्यास्तांस्तितिक्षस्व भारत।।2.14।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

When the senses meet the world, we feel heat and cold, comfort and pain. These experiences arrive and pass away. Krishna asks Arjuna to bear their changes with patience.
