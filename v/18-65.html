Bhagavad Gita 18.65

मन्मना भव मद्भक्तो मद्याजी मां नमस्कुरु।मामेवैष्यसि सत्यं ते प्रतिजाने प्रियोऽसि मे।।18.65।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Fix your mind on me, be my devotee, worship me and bow to me. You will come to me alone; I truly promise you this, for you are dear to me.
