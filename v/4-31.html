Bhagavad Gita 4.31

यज्ञशिष्टामृतभुजो यान्ति ब्रह्म सनातनम्। नायं लोकोऽस्त्ययज्ञस्य कुतो़ऽन्यः कुरुसत्तम।।4.31।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Those who eat what remains after offering reach the eternal. Even this world is not for the one who offers nothing, so how could another?
