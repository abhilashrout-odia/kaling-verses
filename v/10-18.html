Bhagavad Gita 10.18

विस्तरेणात्मनो योगं विभूतिं च जनार्दन। भूयः कथय तृप्तिर्हि श्रृण्वतो नास्ति मेऽमृतम्।।10.18।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Krishna, tell me again in detail of your yoga and manifestations. I am never satisfied with hearing your nectar-like words.
