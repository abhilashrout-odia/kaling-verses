Bhagavad Gita 1.23

योत्स्यमानानवेक्षेऽहं य एतेऽत्र समागताः। धार्तराष्ट्रस्य दुर्बुद्धेर्युद्धे प्रियचिकीर्षवः।।1.23।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

I want to look at those gathered here who are eager to please the wicked-minded son of Dhritarashtra by fighting.
