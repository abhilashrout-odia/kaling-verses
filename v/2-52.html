Bhagavad Gita 2.52

यदा ते मोहकलिलं बुद्धिर्व्यतितरिष्यति। तदा गन्तासि निर्वेदं श्रोतव्यस्य श्रुतस्य च।।2.52।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

When Arjuna's understanding passes beyond the confusion of delusion, Krishna says he will become detached from what he has heard and what he has yet to hear.
