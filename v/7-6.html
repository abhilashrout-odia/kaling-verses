Bhagavad Gita 7.6

एतद्योनीनि भूतानि सर्वाणीत्युपधारय। अहं कृत्स्नस्य जगतः प्रभवः प्रलयस्तथा।।7.6।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Know that all beings arise from these two. I am the source of the whole world and also where it dissolves.
