Bhagavad Gita 10.29

अनन्तश्चास्मि नागानां वरुणो यादसामहम्। पितृ़णामर्यमा चास्मि यमः संयमतामहम्।।10.29।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Among Nagas I am Ananta; among water beings, Varuna. Among ancestors I am Aryaman; among those who restrain, Yama.
