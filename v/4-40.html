Bhagavad Gita 4.40

अज्ञश्चाश्रद्दधानश्च संशयात्मा विनश्यति। नायं लोकोऽस्ति न परो न सुखं संशयात्मनः।।4.40।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

One who is ignorant, without faith and of a doubting nature perishes. For the doubting self there is no world, neither this one nor the next, and no happiness.
