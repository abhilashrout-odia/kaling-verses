Bhagavad Gita 4.4

अर्जुन उवाच अपरं भवतो जन्म परं जन्म विवस्वतः। कथमेतद्विजानीयां त्वमादौ प्रोक्तवानिति।।4.4।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Arjuna said: Your birth was later, and Vivasvan's was long before. How am I to understand that you taught this at the beginning?
