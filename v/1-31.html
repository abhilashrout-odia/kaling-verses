Bhagavad Gita 1.31

निमित्तानि च पश्यामि विपरीतानि केशव। न च श्रेयोऽनुपश्यामि हत्वा स्वजनमाहवे।।1.31।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Arjuna says he sees bad omens, and he cannot see any good coming from killing his own people in battle.
