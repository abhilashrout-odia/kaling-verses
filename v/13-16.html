Bhagavad Gita 13.15

बहिरन्तश्च भूतानामचरं चरमेव च।सूक्ष्मत्वात्तदविज्ञेयं दूरस्थं चान्तिके च तत्।।13.16।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

It is outside and inside beings, the unmoving and the moving; because it is subtle, it cannot be known; it is far away and also near.
