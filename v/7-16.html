Bhagavad Gita 7.16

चतुर्विधा भजन्ते मां जनाः सुकृतिनोऽर्जुन। आर्तो जिज्ञासुरर्थार्थी ज्ञानी च भरतर्षभ।।7.16।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Four kinds of good people worship me, Arjuna: the one in distress, the one seeking knowledge, the one seeking wealth, and the wise.
