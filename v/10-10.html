Bhagavad Gita 10.10

तेषां सततयुक्तानां भजतां प्रीतिपूर्वकम्। ददामि बुद्धियोगं तं येन मामुपयान्ति ते।।10.10।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

To those constantly devoted and worshipping me with love, I give the yoga of understanding by which they come to me.
