Bhagavad Gita 4.2

एवं परम्पराप्राप्तमिमं राजर्षयो विदुः। स कालेनेह महता योगो नष्टः परन्तप।।4.2।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Passed down in this way, the royal sages knew it. But over a long time, this yoga was lost here, Arjuna.
