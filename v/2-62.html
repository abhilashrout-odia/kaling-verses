Bhagavad Gita 2.62

ध्यायतो विषयान्पुंसः सङ्गस्तेषूपजायते। सङ्गात् संजायते कामः कामात्क्रोधोऽभिजायते।।2.62।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Dwelling on sense objects gives rise to attachment. From attachment grows desire, and from desire arises anger.
