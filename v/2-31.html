Bhagavad Gita 2.31

स्वधर्ममपि चावेक्ष्य न विकम्पितुमर्हसि। धर्म्याद्धि युद्धाछ्रेयोऽन्यत्क्षत्रियस्य न विद्यते।।2.31।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Krishna now turns to Arjuna's duty as a warrior. He says Arjuna should not waver, because for a Kshatriya there is no higher duty than a righteous battle.
