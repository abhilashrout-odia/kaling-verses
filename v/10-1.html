Bhagavad Gita 10.1

श्री भगवानुवाच भूय एव महाबाहो श्रृणु मे परमं वचः। यत्तेऽहं प्रीयमाणाय वक्ष्यामि हितकाम्यया।।10.1।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

The Lord said: Hear again my highest word, mighty-armed Arjuna. I shall speak it to you, who delight in it, wishing your good.
