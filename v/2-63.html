Bhagavad Gita 2.63

क्रोधाद्भवति सम्मोहः सम्मोहात्स्मृतिविभ्रमः। स्मृतिभ्रंशाद्बुद्धिनाशो बुद्धिनाशात्प्रणश्यति॥

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Anger clouds the mind and makes us forget what we know. When we lose that wisdom, our judgment fails and we can harm ourselves.
