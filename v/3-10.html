Bhagavad Gita 3.10

सहयज्ञाः प्रजाः सृष्ट्वा पुरोवाच प्रजापतिः। अनेन प्रसविष्यध्वमेष वोऽस्त्विष्टकामधुक्।।3.10।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

In the beginning the creator made people together with offering (yajna) and said: grow through this, and let it be the giver of what you rightly wish for.
