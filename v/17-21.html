Bhagavad Gita 17.21

यत्तु प्रत्युपकारार्थं फलमुद्दिश्य वा पुनः।दीयते च परिक्लिष्टं तद्दानं राजसं स्मृतम्।।17.21।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

But a gift given in expectation of something in return, or looking for a reward, or given reluctantly, is held to be rajasic.
