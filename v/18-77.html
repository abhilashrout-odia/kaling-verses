Bhagavad Gita 18.77

तच्च संस्मृत्य संस्मृत्य रूपमत्यद्भुतं हरेः। विस्मयो मे महान् राजन् हृष्यामि च पुनः पुनः।।18.77।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

And repeatedly recalling that most wondrous form of Krishna, O king, I am filled with great wonder and rejoice again and again.
