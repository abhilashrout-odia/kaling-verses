Bhagavad Gita 3.33

सदृशं चेष्टते स्वस्याः प्रकृतेर्ज्ञानवानपि। प्रकृतिं यान्ति भूतानि निग्रहः किं करिष्यति।।3.33।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Even a wise person acts in line with their own nature. All beings follow their nature. What can forcing it achieve?
