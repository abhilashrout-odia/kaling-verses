Bhagavad Gita 4.1

श्री भगवानुवाच इमं विवस्वते योगं प्रोक्तवानहमव्ययम्। विवस्वान् मनवे प्राह मनुरिक्ष्वाकवेऽब्रवीत्।।4.1।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

The Blessed Lord said: I taught this imperishable yoga to Vivasvan, the sun. He passed it to Manu, and Manu passed it to Ikshvaku.
