Bhagavad Gita 18.68

य इमं परमं गुह्यं मद्भक्तेष्वभिधास्यति।भक्तिं मयि परां कृत्वा मामेवैष्यत्यसंशयः।।18.68।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Whoever teaches this supreme secret among my devotees, having offered highest devotion to me, will come to me alone, without doubt.
