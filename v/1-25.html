Bhagavad Gita 1.25

भीष्मद्रोणप्रमुखतः सर्वेषां च महीक्षिताम्। उवाच पार्थ पश्यैतान्समवेतान्कुरूनिति।।1.25।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

In front of Bhishma, Drona and all the kings, he said: Arjuna, look at these Kurus gathered here.
