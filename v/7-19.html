Bhagavad Gita 7.19

बहूनां जन्मनामन्ते ज्ञानवान्मां प्रपद्यते। वासुदेवः सर्वमिति स महात्मा सुदुर्लभः।।7.19।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

At the end of many births, the wise one comes to me, knowing 'Vasudeva is everything'. Such a great soul is very rare.
