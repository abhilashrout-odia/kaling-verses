Bhagavad Gita 7.2

ज्ञानं तेऽहं सविज्ञानमिदं वक्ष्याम्यशेषतः। यज्ज्ञात्वा नेह भूयोऽन्यज्ज्ञातव्यमवशिष्यते।।7.2।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

I will explain to you this knowledge together with its realization, completely. When you know it, nothing more here remains to be known.
