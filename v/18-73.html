Bhagavad Gita 18.73

अर्जुन उवाचनष्टो मोहः स्मृतिर्लब्धा त्वत्प्रसादान्मयाच्युत।स्थितोऽस्मि गतसन्देहः करिष्ये वचनं तव।।18.73।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Arjuna said: My delusion is destroyed; by your grace, Krishna, I have regained my memory. I stand firm, free of doubt. I shall act according to your word.
