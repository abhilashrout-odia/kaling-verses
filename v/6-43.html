Bhagavad Gita 6.43

तत्र तं बुद्धिसंयोगं लभते पौर्वदेहिकम्। यतते च ततो भूयः संसिद्धौ कुरुनन्दन।।6.43।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

There he regains the understanding he gained in his earlier life, and from there he strives again toward perfection, Arjuna.
