Bhagavad Gita 4.15

एवं ज्ञात्वा कृतं कर्म पूर्वैरपि मुमुक्षुभिः। कुरु कर्मैव तस्मात्त्वं पूर्वैः पूर्वतरं कृतम्।।4.15।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Knowing this, even seekers of the past acted. So you also act, as the ancients did before you.
