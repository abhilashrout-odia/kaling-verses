Bhagavad Gita 4.25

दैवमेवापरे यज्ञं योगिनः पर्युपासते। ब्रह्माग्नावपरे यज्ञं यज्ञेनैवोपजुह्वति।।4.25।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Some practitioners perform the offering (yajna) of worship to the powers (devas). Others offer the offering itself into the fire of Brahman.
