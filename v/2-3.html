Bhagavad Gita 2.3

क्लैब्यं मा स्म गमः पार्थ नैतत्त्वय्युपपद्यते। क्षुद्रं हृदयदौर्बल्यं त्यक्त्वोत्तिष्ठ परन्तप।।2.3।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Krishna urges Arjuna not to give in to helplessness. He asks him to set aside this weakness of heart and stand up to face the battle.
