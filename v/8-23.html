Bhagavad Gita 8.23

यत्र काले त्वनावृत्तिमावृत्तिं चैव योगिनः। प्रयाता यान्ति तं कालं वक्ष्यामि भरतर्षभ।।8.23।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Now I will tell you, Arjuna, the times at which yogis who depart do not return, and those at which they do return.
