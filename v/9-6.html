Bhagavad Gita 9.6

यथाऽऽकाशस्थितो नित्यं वायुः सर्वत्रगो महान्। तथा सर्वाणि भूतानि मत्स्थानीत्युपधारय।।9.6।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

As the mighty wind moves everywhere while always remaining in space, understand that all beings likewise abide in me.
