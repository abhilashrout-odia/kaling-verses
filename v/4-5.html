Bhagavad Gita 4.5

श्री भगवानुवाच बहूनि मे व्यतीतानि जन्मानि तव चार्जुन। तान्यहं वेद सर्वाणि न त्वं वेत्थ परन्तप।।4.5।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

The Blessed Lord said: Many births of mine and yours have passed, Arjuna. I know them all, but you do not.
