Bhagavad Gita 10.35

बृहत्साम तथा साम्नां गायत्री छन्दसामहम्। मासानां मार्गशीर्षोऽहमृतूनां कुसुमाकरः।।10.35।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Among Sama hymns I am the Brihat Sama; among metres, Gayatri. Among months I am Margashirsha; among seasons, flower-bearing spring.
