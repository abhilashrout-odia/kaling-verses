Bhagavad Gita 3.2

व्यामिश्रेणेव वाक्येन बुद्धिं मोहयसीव मे। तदेकं वद निश्चित्य येन श्रेयोऽहमाप्नुयाम्।।3.2।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Your mixed-sounding words seem to confuse my mind. Tell me one thing clearly, by which I may reach what is truly good for me.
