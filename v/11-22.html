Bhagavad Gita 11.22

रुद्रादित्या वसवो ये च साध्या विश्वेऽश्विनौ मरुतश्चोष्मपाश्च। गन्धर्वयक्षासुरसिद्धसङ्घा वीक्षन्ते त्वां विस्मिताश्चैव सर्वे।।11.22।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Rudras, Adityas, Vasus, Sadhyas, Vishvadevas, the two Ashvins, Maruts, ancestors who receive warm offerings, and hosts of Gandharvas, Yakshas, Asuras and perfected beings all gaze at you in wonder.
