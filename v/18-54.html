Bhagavad Gita 18.54

ब्रह्मभूतः प्रसन्नात्मा न शोचति न काङ्क्षति।समः सर्वेषु भूतेषु मद्भक्तिं लभते पराम्।।18.54।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Having become Brahman, with a serene self, he neither grieves nor craves; the same toward all beings, he gains supreme devotion to me.
