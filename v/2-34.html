Bhagavad Gita 2.34

अकीर्तिं चापि भूतानि कथयिष्यन्ति तेऽव्ययाम्। संभावितस्य चाकीर्तिर्मरणादतिरिच्यते।।2.34।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Krishna tells Arjuna that people will speak of his lasting dishonor. For someone held in high regard, he says dishonor is worse than death.
