Bhagavad Gita 2.58

यदा संहरते चायं कूर्मोऽङ्गानीव सर्वशः। इन्द्रियाणीन्द्रियार्थेभ्यस्तस्य प्रज्ञा प्रतिष्ठिता।।2.58।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Like a tortoise drawing in its limbs, a person whose senses withdraw from their objects has steady wisdom.
