Bhagavad Gita 2.43

कामात्मानः स्वर्गपरा जन्मकर्मफलप्रदाम्। क्रियाविशेषबहुलां भोगैश्वर्यगतिं प्रति।।2.43।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Continuing the previous verse, Krishna describes people driven by desire who make heaven their goal. Their teachings promise rebirth as the reward of actions and prescribe many rituals to gain pleasure and power.
