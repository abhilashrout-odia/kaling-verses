Bhagavad Gita 4.8

परित्राणाय साधूनां विनाशाय च दुष्कृताम्। धर्मसंस्थापनार्थाय संभवामि युगे युगे।।4.8।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Krishna says he appears age after age to safeguard the good, bring the doers of evil to destruction, and restore dharma.
