Bhagavad Gita 3.11

देवान्भावयतानेन ते देवा भावयन्तु वः। परस्परं भावयन्तः श्रेयः परमवाप्स्यथ।।3.11।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Nourish the gods through this offering (yajna), and may the gods nourish you. By supporting one another in this way, you will reach the highest good.
