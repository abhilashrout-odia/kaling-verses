Bhagavad Gita 2.36

अवाच्यवादांश्च बहून् वदिष्यन्ति तवाहिताः। निन्दन्तस्तव सामर्थ्यं ततो दुःखतरं नु किम्।।2.36।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Krishna says Arjuna's enemies will insult him and mock his ability. He asks what could be more painful for Arjuna than that.
