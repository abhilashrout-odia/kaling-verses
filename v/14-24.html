Bhagavad Gita 14.24

समदुःखसुखः स्वस्थः समलोष्टाश्मकाञ्चनः।तुल्यप्रियाप्रियो धीरस्तुल्यनिन्दात्मसंस्तुतिः।।14.24।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

same in pleasure and pain, steady in himself, regarding a clod, a stone and gold alike, the same toward dear and not dear, steadfast, the same in blame and in his own praise,
