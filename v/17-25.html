Bhagavad Gita 17.25

तदित्यनभिसन्धाय फलं यज्ञतपःक्रियाः।दानक्रियाश्च विविधाः क्रियन्ते मोक्षकाङ्क्षि।।17.25।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Saying 'Tat', without aiming at reward, the various acts of offering, austerity and giving are done by those who seek liberation.
