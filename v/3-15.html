Bhagavad Gita 3.15

कर्म ब्रह्मोद्भवं विद्धि ब्रह्माक्षरसमुद्भवम्। तस्मात्सर्वगतं ब्रह्म नित्यं यज्ञे प्रतिष्ठितम्।।3.15।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Action comes from the sacred word, and the sacred word comes from the imperishable. So the all-pervading is always present in offering (yajna).
