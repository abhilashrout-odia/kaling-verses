Bhagavad Gita 10.42

अथवा बहुनैतेन किं ज्ञातेन तवार्जुन। विष्टभ्याहमिदं कृत्स्नमेकांशेन स्थितो जगत्।।10.42।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Or what use is all this detailed knowledge to you, Arjuna? Sustaining this entire world with a single portion of myself, I remain.
