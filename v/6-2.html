Bhagavad Gita 6.2

यं संन्यासमिति प्राहुर्योगं तं विद्धि पाण्डव। न ह्यसंन्यस्तसङ्कल्पो योगी भवति कश्चन।।6.2।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Know that what they call renunciation (sannyasa) is yoga, Arjuna; for no one becomes a yogi without giving up their resolves (sankalpa).
