Bhagavad Gita 9.28

शुभाशुभफलैरेवं मोक्ष्यसे कर्मबन्धनैः। संन्यासयोगयुक्तात्मा विमुक्तो मामुपैष्यसि।।9.28।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Thus you will be freed from the bonds of action and their good and bad results. Joined to the yoga of renunciation, liberated, you will come to me.
