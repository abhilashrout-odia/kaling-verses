Bhagavad Gita 5.17

तद्बुद्धयस्तदात्मानस्तन्निष्ठास्तत्परायणाः। गच्छन्त्यपुनरावृत्तिं ज्ञाननिर्धूतकल्मषाः।।5.17।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

With their minds and selves set on That, established in That, devoted to That, they go where there is no return, their wrongs washed away by knowledge.
