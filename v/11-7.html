Bhagavad Gita 11.7

इहैकस्थं जगत्कृत्स्नं पश्याद्य सचराचरम्। मम देहे गुडाकेश यच्चान्यद्द्रष्टुमिच्छसि।।11.7।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Now see the whole world, moving and unmoving, gathered in one place within my body, Arjuna, and whatever else you wish to see.
