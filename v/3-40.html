Bhagavad Gita 3.40

इन्द्रियाणि मनो बुद्धिरस्याधिष्ठानमुच्यते। एतैर्विमोहयत्येष ज्ञानमावृत्य देहिनम्।।3.40।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

The senses, the mind and the intellect are said to be its home. Through them it covers knowledge and deludes the embodied person.
