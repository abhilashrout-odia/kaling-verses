Bhagavad Gita 2.37

हतो वा प्राप्स्यसि स्वर्गं जित्वा वा भोक्ष्यसे महीम्। तस्मादुत्तिष्ठ कौन्तेय युद्धाय कृतनिश्चयः।।2.37।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Krishna tells Arjuna that if he dies in battle he will reach heaven, and if he wins he will enjoy the earth. He therefore asks him to rise with the resolve to fight.
