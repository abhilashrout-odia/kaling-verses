Bhagavad Gita 2.4

अर्जुन उवाच कथं भीष्ममहं संख्ये द्रोणं च मधुसूदन। इषुभिः प्रतियोत्स्यामि पूजार्हावरिसूदन।।2.4।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Arjuna asks how he can aim his arrows at Bhishma and Drona in battle. They are elders and teachers whom he deeply respects.
