Bhagavad Gita 7.4

भूमिरापोऽनलो वायुः खं मनो बुद्धिरेव च। अहङ्कार इतीयं मे भिन्ना प्रकृतिरष्टधा।।7.4।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Earth, water, fire, air, space, mind, intellect and the sense of 'I': this is how my nature is divided in eight.
