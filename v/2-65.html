Bhagavad Gita 2.65

प्रसादे सर्वदुःखानां हानिरस्योपजायते। प्रसन्नचेतसो ह्याशु बुद्धिः पर्यवतिष्ठते।।2.65।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Krishna says that in this serenity, all sorrows fall away and the understanding of a tranquil mind soon becomes steady.
