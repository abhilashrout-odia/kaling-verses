Bhagavad Gita 10.28

आयुधानामहं वज्रं धेनूनामस्मि कामधुक्। प्रजनश्चास्मि कन्दर्पः सर्पाणामस्मि वासुकिः।।10.28।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Among weapons I am the thunderbolt; among cows, the wish-fulfilling cow. I am Kandarpa, the cause of procreation; among serpents, Vasuki.
