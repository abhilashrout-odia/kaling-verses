Bhagavad Gita 12.14

सन्तुष्टः सततं योगी यतात्मा दृढनिश्चयः।मय्यर्पितमनोबुद्धिर्यो मद्भक्तः स मे प्रियः।।12.14।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

always content, a yogi, self-controlled, firm in resolve, with mind and understanding offered to me - such a devotee of mine is dear to me.
