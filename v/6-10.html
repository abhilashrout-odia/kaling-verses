Bhagavad Gita 6.10

योगी युञ्जीत सततमात्मानं रहसि स्थितः। एकाकी यतचित्तात्मा निराशीरपरिग्रहः।।6.10।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

A yogi should keep steadying the mind, staying alone in a quiet place, with mind and body controlled, free from craving and from holding on to possessions.

Hindi (AI drafted, not reviewed)
