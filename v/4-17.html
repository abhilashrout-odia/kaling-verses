Bhagavad Gita 4.17

कर्मणो ह्यपि बोद्धव्यं बोद्धव्यं च विकर्मणः। अकर्मणश्च बोद्धव्यं गहना कर्मणो गतिः।।4.17।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

One must understand right action, wrong action, and also inaction. The course of action is hard to see into.
