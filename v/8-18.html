Bhagavad Gita 8.18

अव्यक्ताद्व्यक्तयः सर्वाः प्रभवन्त्यहरागमे। रात्र्यागमे प्रलीयन्ते तत्रैवाव्यक्तसंज्ञके।।8.18।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

From the unseen all visible things come forth when day begins, and when night falls they dissolve again into that same unseen.
