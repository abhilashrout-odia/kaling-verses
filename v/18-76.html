Bhagavad Gita 18.76

राजन्संस्मृत्य संस्मृत्य संवादमिममद्भुतम्।केशवार्जुनयोः पुण्यं हृष्यामि च मुहुर्मुहुः।।18.76।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

O king, repeatedly recalling this wondrous and holy dialogue between Krishna and Arjuna, I rejoice again and again.
