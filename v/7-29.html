Bhagavad Gita 7.29

जरामरणमोक्षाय मामाश्रित्य यतन्ति ये। ते ब्रह्म तद्विदुः कृत्स्नमध्यात्मं कर्म चाखिलम्।।7.29।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Those who take refuge in me and strive to be free from old age and death come to know that Brahman, the whole of the inner self, and all of action.
