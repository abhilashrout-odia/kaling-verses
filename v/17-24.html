Bhagavad Gita 17.24

तस्मादोमित्युदाहृत्य यज्ञदानतपःक्रियाः।प्रवर्तन्ते विधानोक्ताः सततं ब्रह्मवादिनाम्।।17.24।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Therefore, uttering 'Om', the acts of offering, giving and austerity prescribed by the rules are always begun by those who speak of Brahman.
