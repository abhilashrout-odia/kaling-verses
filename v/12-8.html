Bhagavad Gita 12.8

मय्येव मन आधत्स्व मयि बुद्धिं निवेशय।निवसिष्यसि मय्येव अत ऊर्ध्वं न संशयः।।12.8।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Fix your mind on me alone; place your understanding in me. Then you will live in me alone, from now on. Of this there is no doubt.
