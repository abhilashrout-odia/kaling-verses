Bhagavad Gita 8.19

भूतग्रामः स एवायं भूत्वा भूत्वा प्रलीयते। रात्र्यागमेऽवशः पार्थ प्रभवत्यहरागमे।।8.19।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

This same crowd of beings, coming into being again and again, dissolves helplessly at nightfall and arises again at daybreak, Arjuna.
