Bhagavad Gita 2.68

तस्माद्यस्य महाबाहो निगृहीतानि सर्वशः। इन्द्रियाणीन्द्रियार्थेभ्यस्तस्य प्रज्ञा प्रतिष्ठिता।।2.68।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Krishna concludes that a person whose senses are fully restrained from their objects has steady wisdom.
