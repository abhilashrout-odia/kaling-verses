Bhagavad Gita 1.37

तस्मान्नार्हा वयं हन्तुं धार्तराष्ट्रान्स्वबान्धवान्। स्वजनं हि कथं हत्वा सुखिनः स्याम माधव।।1.37।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

So we should not kill our own relatives, the sons of Dhritarashtra. How can we be happy, Krishna, after killing our own people?
