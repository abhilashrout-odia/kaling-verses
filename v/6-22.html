Bhagavad Gita 6.22

यं लब्ध्वा चापरं लाभं मन्यते नाधिकं ततः। यस्मिन्स्थितो न दुःखेन गुरुणापि विचाल्यते।।6.22।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Having gained it, one thinks no other gain is greater, and established in it, one is not shaken even by great sorrow.
