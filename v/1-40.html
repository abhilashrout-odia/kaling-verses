Bhagavad Gita 1.40

कुलक्षये प्रणश्यन्ति कुलधर्माः सनातनाः। धर्मे नष्टे कुलं कृत्स्नमधर्मोऽभिभवत्युत।।1.40।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

When a family is destroyed, its age-old ways of dharma fade. When dharma is lost, disorder overcomes the whole family.
