Bhagavad Gita 8.1

अर्जुन उवाच किं तद्ब्रह्म किमध्यात्मं किं कर्म पुरुषोत्तम। अधिभूतं च किं प्रोक्तमधिदैवं किमुच्यते।।8.1।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Arjuna said: What is that Brahman, Krishna? What is the self-related (adhyatma), what is action (karma), what is the realm of beings (adhibhuta), and what is the realm of the divine (adhidaiva)?
