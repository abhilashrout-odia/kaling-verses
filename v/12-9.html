Bhagavad Gita 12.9

अथ चित्तं समाधातुं न शक्नोषि मयि स्थिरम्।अभ्यासयोगेन ततो मामिच्छाप्तुं धनञ्जय।।12.9।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

If you cannot hold your mind steady on me, then seek to reach me through the yoga of practice (abhyasa), Arjuna.
