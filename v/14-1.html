Bhagavad Gita 14.1

श्री भगवानुवाचपरं भूयः प्रवक्ष्यामि ज्ञानानां ज्ञानमुत्तमम्।यज्ज्ञात्वा मुनयः सर्वे परां सिद्धिमितो गताः।।14.1।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

The Blessed Lord said: I will tell you again the highest of all knowledge, knowing which all the sages went from here to supreme perfection.
