Bhagavad Gita 17.16

मनःप्रसादः सौम्यत्वं मौनमात्मविनिग्रहः।भावसंशुद्धिरित्येतत्तपो मानसमुच्यते।।17.16।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Peace of mind, gentleness, silence, self-control and purity of feeling - this is called austerity of the mind.
