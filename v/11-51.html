Bhagavad Gita 11.51

अर्जुन उवाच दृष्ट्वेदं मानुषं रूपं तवसौम्यं जनार्दन। इदानीमस्मि संवृत्तः सचेताः प्रकृतिं गतः।।11.51।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Arjuna said: Krishna, seeing this gentle human form of yours, I am now composed and have returned to my own state.
