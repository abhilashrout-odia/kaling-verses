Bhagavad Gita 10.8

अहं सर्वस्य प्रभवो मत्तः सर्वं प्रवर्तते। इति मत्वा भजन्ते मां बुधा भावसमन्विताः।।10.8।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

I am the source of everything; everything proceeds from me. Knowing this, the wise worship me with heartfelt devotion.
