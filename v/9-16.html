Bhagavad Gita 9.16

अहं क्रतुरहं यज्ञः स्वधाऽहमहमौषधम्। मंत्रोऽहमहमेवाज्यमहमग्निरहं हुतम्।।9.16।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

I am the Vedic rite; I am the offering (yajna). I am the ancestral oblation and the herb. I am the mantra, the clarified butter, the fire and the offering made into it.
