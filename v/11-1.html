Bhagavad Gita 11.1

अर्जुन उवाच मदनुग्रहाय परमं गुह्यमध्यात्मसंज्ञितम्। यत्त्वयोक्तं वचस्तेन मोहोऽयं विगतो मम।।11.1।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Arjuna said: Through the supremely secret teaching called knowledge of the Self, which you spoke for my sake, this delusion of mine has gone.
