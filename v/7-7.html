Bhagavad Gita 7.7

मत्तः परतरं नान्यत्किञ्चिदस्ति धनञ्जय। मयि सर्वमिदं प्रोतं सूत्रे मणिगणा इव।।7.7।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Nothing is higher than me, Arjuna. All of this is strung on me, like rows of pearls on a thread.
