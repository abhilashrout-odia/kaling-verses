Bhagavad Gita 18.57

चेतसा सर्वकर्माणि मयि संन्यस्य मत्परः।बुद्धियोगमुपाश्रित्य मच्चित्तः सततं भव।।18.57।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Mentally placing all your actions in me, devoted to me, relying on the yoga of understanding (buddhi-yoga), keep your mind always on me.
