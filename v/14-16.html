Bhagavad Gita 14.16

कर्मणः सुकृतस्याहुः सात्त्विकं निर्मलं फलम्।रजसस्तु फलं दुःखमज्ञानं तमसः फलम्।।14.16।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

They say the fruit of well-done action is sattvic and pure; the fruit of rajas is pain, and the fruit of tamas is ignorance.
