Bhagavad Gita 6.28

युञ्जन्नेवं सदाऽऽत्मानं योगी विगतकल्मषः। सुखेन ब्रह्मसंस्पर्शमत्यन्तं सुखमश्नुते।।6.28।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Always engaging the self in this way, the yogi, free from wrong, easily enjoys the endless happiness of touching Brahman.
