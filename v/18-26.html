Bhagavad Gita 18.26

मुक्तसङ्गोऽनहंवादी धृत्युत्साहसमन्वितः।सिद्ध्यसिद्ध्योर्निर्विकारः कर्ता सात्त्विक उच्यते।।18.26।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

A doer free of attachment, not boastful, with steadiness and energy, unchanged in success or failure, is called sattvic.
