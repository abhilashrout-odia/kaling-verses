Bhagavad Gita 6.38

कच्चिन्नोभयविभ्रष्टश्छिन्नाभ्रमिव नश्यति। अप्रतिष्ठो महाबाहो विमूढो ब्रह्मणः पथि।।6.38।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Fallen from both paths, without support, bewildered on the path to Brahman, does that person perish like a torn cloud, Krishna?
