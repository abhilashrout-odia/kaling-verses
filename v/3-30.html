Bhagavad Gita 3.30

मयि सर्वाणि कर्माणि संन्यस्याध्यात्मचेतसा। निराशीर्निर्ममो भूत्वा युध्यस्व विगतज्वरः।।3.30।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Offer all your actions to me, with your mind resting on the Self. Free from craving and possessiveness, and without inner fever, go and fight.
