Bhagavad Gita 11.43

पितासि लोकस्य चराचरस्य त्वमस्य पूज्यश्च गुरुर्गरीयान्। न त्वत्समोऽस्त्यभ्यधिकः कुतोऽन्यो लोकत्रयेऽप्यप्रतिमप्रभाव।।11.43।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

You are the father of the moving and unmoving world, its worthy object of worship and its greater teacher. None equals you; how could anyone be greater, even in the three worlds, one of incomparable power?
