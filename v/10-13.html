Bhagavad Gita 10.13

आहुस्त्वामृषयः सर्वे देवर्षिर्नारदस्तथा। असितो देवलो व्यासः स्वयं चैव ब्रवीषि मे।।10.13।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

All the sages declare this of you, including the divine sage Narada, Asita, Devala and Vyasa; and you yourself tell me so.
