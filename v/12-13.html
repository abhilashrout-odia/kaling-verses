Bhagavad Gita 12.13

अद्वेष्टा सर्वभूतानां मैत्रः करुण एव च।निर्ममो निरहङ्कारः समदुःखसुखः क्षमी।।12.13।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

One who hates no being, is friendly and compassionate, free of 'mine' and of egoism, the same in pain and pleasure, and forgiving,
