Bhagavad Gita 6.12

तत्रैकाग्रं मनः कृत्वा यतचित्तेन्द्रियक्रियः। उपविश्यासने युञ्ज्याद्योगमात्मविशुद्धये।।6.12।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Sitting there, with the mind made one-pointed and thoughts and senses held in check, one should practise yoga for the purification of the self.

Hindi (AI drafted, not reviewed)
