Bhagavad Gita 6.11

शुचौ देशे प्रतिष्ठाप्य स्थिरमासनमात्मनः। नात्युच्छ्रितं नातिनीचं चैलाजिनकुशोत्तरम्।।6.11।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

In a clean place, one should set up a firm seat for oneself, not too high and not too low, covered with cloth, deerskin and kusha grass.
