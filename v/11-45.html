Bhagavad Gita 11.45

अदृष्टपूर्वं हृषितोऽस्मि दृष्ट्वा भयेन च प्रव्यथितं मनो मे। तदेव मे दर्शय देव रूपं प्रसीद देवेश जगन्निवास।।11.45।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

I rejoice at seeing what has never been seen before, yet my mind is shaken with fear. Show me that former form, Lord. Be gracious, Lord of gods, dwelling of the world.
