Bhagavad Gita 12.20

ये तु धर्म्यामृतमिदं यथोक्तं पर्युपासते।श्रद्दधाना मत्परमा भक्तास्तेऽतीव मे प्रियाः।।12.20।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

But those who follow this nectar of dharma, as it has been taught, with faith, holding me as their highest aim - those devotees are exceedingly dear to me.
