Bhagavad Gita 10.12

अर्जुन उवाच परं ब्रह्म परं धाम पवित्रं परमं भवान्। पुरुषं शाश्वतं दिव्यमादिदेवमजं विभुम्।।10.12।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Arjuna said: You are the supreme Brahman, the highest dwelling and the highest purifier; the eternal divine Person, the first god, unborn and all-pervading.
