Bhagavad Gita 15.12

यदादित्यगतं तेजो जगद्भासयतेऽखिलम्।यच्चन्द्रमसि यच्चाग्नौ तत्तेजो विद्धि मामकम्।।15.12।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Know that the brilliance in the sun that lights the whole world, and that in the moon and in fire, is mine.
