Bhagavad Gita 7.20

कामैस्तैस्तैर्हृतज्ञानाः प्रपद्यन्तेऽन्यदेवताः। तं तं नियममास्थाय प्रकृत्या नियताः स्वया।।7.20।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Those whose understanding is carried away by various desires turn to other deities, following different rules, driven by their own nature.
