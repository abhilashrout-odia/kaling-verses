Bhagavad Gita 4.38

न हि ज्ञानेन सदृशं पवित्रमिह विद्यते। तत्स्वयं योगसंसिद्धः कालेनात्मनि विन्दति।।4.38।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Nothing in this world purifies like knowledge. One who is perfected through practice finds it within, in time.
