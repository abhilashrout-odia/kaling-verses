Bhagavad Gita 6.47

योगिनामपि सर्वेषां मद्गतेनान्तरात्मना। श्रद्धावान्भजते यो मां स मे युक्ततमो मतः।।6.47।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Of all yogis, the one who, with faith and inner self absorbed in me, worships me, is in my view the most deeply joined to yoga.
