Bhagavad Gita 15.10

उत्क्रामन्तं स्थितं वापि भुञ्जानं वा गुणान्वितम्।विमूढा नानुपश्यन्ति पश्यन्ति ज्ञानचक्षुषः।।15.10।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

The deluded do not see him as he departs, stays or enjoys, joined with the gunas; those with the eye of knowledge see.
