Bhagavad Gita 7.26

वेदाहं समतीतानि वर्तमानानि चार्जुन। भविष्याणि च भूतानि मां तु वेद न कश्चन।।7.26।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

I know the beings of the past, the present and the future, Arjuna, but no one knows me.
