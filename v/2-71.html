Bhagavad Gita 2.71

विहाय कामान्यः सर्वान्पुमांश्चरति निःस्पृहः। निर्ममो निरहंकारः स शांतिमधिगच्छति।।2.71।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

A person who gives up desires and lives without craving, possessiveness or the sense of ego attains peace.
