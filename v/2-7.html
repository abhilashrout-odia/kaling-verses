Bhagavad Gita 2.7

कार्पण्यदोषोपहतस्वभावः पृच्छामि त्वां धर्मसंमूढचेताः। यच्छ्रेयः स्यान्निश्चितं ब्रूहि तन्मे शिष्यस्तेऽहं शाधि मां त्वां प्रपन्नम्।।2.7।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Overwhelmed and unsure of his duty, Arjuna asks Krishna to tell him what is truly good. He accepts Krishna as his teacher and asks for guidance.
