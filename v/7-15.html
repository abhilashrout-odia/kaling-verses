Bhagavad Gita 7.15

न मां दुष्कृतिनो मूढाः प्रपद्यन्ते नराधमाः। माययापहृतज्ञाना आसुरं भावमाश्रिताः।।7.15।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Evildoers, the deluded, the lowest of people, whose understanding is carried off by illusion, and who hold a demonic nature, do not take refuge in me.
