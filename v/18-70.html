Bhagavad Gita 18.70

अध्येष्यते च य इमं धर्म्यं संवादमावयोः।ज्ञानयज्ञेन तेनाहमिष्टः स्यामिति मे मतिः।।18.70।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Whoever studies this dialogue of ours in accord with dharma will worship me through the offering (yajna) of knowledge: this is my view.
