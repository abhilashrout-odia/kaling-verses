Bhagavad Gita 2.40

नेहाभिक्रमनाशोऽस्ति प्रत्यवायो न विद्यते। स्वल्पमप्यस्य धर्मस्य त्रायते महतो भयात्।।2.40।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

On this path of disciplined action, sincere effort is not wasted and an incomplete beginning is not a spiritual setback. Even a small practice can offer protection from great fear.
