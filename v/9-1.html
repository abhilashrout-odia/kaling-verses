Bhagavad Gita 9.1

श्री भगवानुवाच इदं तु ते गुह्यतमं प्रवक्ष्याम्यनसूयवे। ज्ञानं विज्ञानसहितं यज्ज्ञात्वा मोक्ष्यसेऽशुभात्।।9.1।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

The Lord said: To you, who do not find fault, I shall explain this most secret knowledge together with its realization. Knowing it, you will be freed from evil.
