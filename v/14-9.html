Bhagavad Gita 14.9

सत्त्वं सुखे सञ्जयति रजः कर्मणि भारत।ज्ञानमावृत्य तु तमः प्रमादे सञ्जयत्युत।।14.9।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Sattva attaches one to happiness, rajas to action, Arjuna; but tamas, covering knowledge, attaches one to carelessness.
