Bhagavad Gita 11.20

द्यावापृथिव्योरिदमन्तरं हि व्याप्तं त्वयैकेन दिशश्च सर्वाः। दृष्ट्वाऽद्भुतं रूपमुग्रं तवेदं लोकत्रयं प्रव्यथितं महात्मन्।।11.20।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

You alone fill the space between heaven and earth and every direction. Great one, the three worlds tremble at seeing this wondrous, fierce form of yours.
