Bhagavad Gita 18.30

प्रवृत्तिं च निवृत्तिं च कार्याकार्ये भयाभये।बन्धं मोक्षं च या वेत्ति बुद्धिः सा पार्थ सात्त्विकी।।18.30।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

The understanding (buddhi) that knows what to engage in and what to turn from, what ought and ought not to be done, what is to be feared and not feared, what binds and what frees, is sattvic, Arjuna.
