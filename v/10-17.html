Bhagavad Gita 10.17

कथं विद्यामहं योगिंस्त्वां सदा परिचिन्तयन्। केषु केषु च भावेषु चिन्त्योऽसि भगवन्मया।।10.17।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

How may I know you, O yogi, while constantly contemplating you? In which forms should I think of you, Lord?
