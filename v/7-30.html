Bhagavad Gita 7.30

साधिभूताधिदैवं मां साधियज्ञं च ये विदुः। प्रयाणकालेऽपि च मां ते विदुर्युक्तचेतसः।।7.30।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Those who know me together with adhibhuta (the realm of beings), adhidaiva (the realm of the divine) and adhiyajna (the one to whom offerings are made) know me with steady mind even at the time of death.
