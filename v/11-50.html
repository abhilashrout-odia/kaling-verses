Bhagavad Gita 11.50

सञ्जय उवाच इत्यर्जुनं वासुदेवस्तथोक्त्वा स्वकं रूपं दर्शयामास भूयः। आश्वासयामास च भीतमेनं भूत्वा पुनः सौम्यवपुर्महात्मा।।11.50।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Sanjaya said: Having spoken thus to Arjuna, Krishna showed his own form again. The great one, becoming gentle in form once more, reassured the frightened Arjuna.
