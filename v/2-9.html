Bhagavad Gita 2.9

सञ्जय उवाच एवमुक्त्वा हृषीकेशं गुडाकेशः परन्तप। न योत्स्य इति गोविन्दमुक्त्वा तूष्णीं बभूव ह।।2.9।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Sanjaya says that, after speaking to Krishna, Arjuna declares that he will not fight. Then he falls silent.
