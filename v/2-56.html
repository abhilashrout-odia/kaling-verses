Bhagavad Gita 2.56

दुःखेष्वनुद्विग्नमनाः सुखेषु विगतस्पृहः। वीतरागभयक्रोधः स्थितधीर्मुनिरुच्यते।।2.56।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

A sage of steady wisdom is described as unshaken by sorrow, without craving for pleasure, and free from attachment, fear and anger.
