Bhagavad Gita 5.11

कायेन मनसा बुद्ध्या केवलैरिन्द्रियैरपि। योगिनः कर्म कुर्वन्ति सङ्गं त्यक्त्वाऽऽत्मशुद्धये।।5.11।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Yogis act with the body, mind, intellect and even the senses, letting go of attachment, in order to purify themselves.
