Bhagavad Gita 1.35

एतान्न हन्तुमिच्छामि घ्नतोऽपि मधुसूदन। अपि त्रैलोक्यराज्यस्य हेतोः किं नु महीकृते।।1.35।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Even if they kill me, Krishna, I do not want to kill them, not even for the rule of the three worlds, let alone for the earth.
