Bhagavad Gita 5.15

नादत्ते कस्यचित्पापं न चैव सुकृतं विभुः। अज्ञानेनावृतं ज्ञानं तेन मुह्यन्ति जन्तवः।।5.15।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

The all-pervading does not take on anyone's wrong or good. Knowledge is covered by ignorance, and so beings are confused.
