Bhagavad Gita 3.36

अर्जुन उवाच अथ केन प्रयुक्तोऽयं पापं चरति पूरुषः। अनिच्छन्नपि वार्ष्णेय बलादिव नियोजितः।।3.36।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Arjuna asks: what drives a person to do wrong even when they do not want to, as if pushed by force?
