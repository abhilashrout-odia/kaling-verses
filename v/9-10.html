Bhagavad Gita 9.10

मयाऽध्यक्षेण प्रकृतिः सूयते सचराचरम्। हेतुनाऽनेन कौन्तेय जगद्विपरिवर्तते।।9.10।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Under my supervision, nature brings forth the moving and the unmoving. Through this, Arjuna, the world goes through its changes.
