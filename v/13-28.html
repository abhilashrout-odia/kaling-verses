Bhagavad Gita 13.27

समं सर्वेषु भूतेषु तिष्ठन्तं परमेश्वरम्।विनश्यत्स्वविनश्यन्तं यः पश्यति स पश्यति।।13.28।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

One who sees the supreme lord standing equally in all beings, the imperishable within the perishing, truly sees.
