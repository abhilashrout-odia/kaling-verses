Bhagavad Gita 11.11

दिव्यमाल्याम्बरधरं दिव्यगन्धानुलेपनम्। सर्वाश्चर्यमयं देवमनन्तं विश्वतोमुखम्।।11.11।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Wearing divine garlands and garments, anointed with divine fragrances, wholly wondrous, the infinite deity with faces everywhere.
