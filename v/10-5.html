Bhagavad Gita 10.5

अहिंसा समता तुष्टिस्तपो दानं यशोऽयशः। भवन्ति भावा भूतानां मत्त एव पृथग्विधाः।।10.5।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Nonviolence, equality, contentment, austerity, giving, fame and disgrace: these diverse states of beings arise from me alone.
