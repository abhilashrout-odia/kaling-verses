Bhagavad Gita 14.3

मम योनिर्महद्ब्रह्म तस्मिन् गर्भं दधाम्यहम्।संभवः सर्वभूतानां ततो भवति भारत।।14.3।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Great Brahman (nature) is my womb; in it I place the seed. From that comes the birth of all beings, Arjuna.
