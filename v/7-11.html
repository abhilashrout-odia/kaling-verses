Bhagavad Gita 7.11

बलं बलवतां चाहं कामरागविवर्जितम्। धर्माविरुद्धो भूतेषु कामोऽस्मि भरतर्षभ।।7.11।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Of the strong, I am the strength that is free of desire and attachment. In beings I am desire that does not go against dharma.
