Bhagavad Gita 11.5

श्री भगवानुवाच पश्य मे पार्थ रूपाणि शतशोऽथ सहस्रशः। नानाविधानि दिव्यानि नानावर्णाकृतीनि च।।11.5।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

The Lord said: Behold my forms, Arjuna, in hundreds and thousands, varied and divine, of many colours and shapes.
