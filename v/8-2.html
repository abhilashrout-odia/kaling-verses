Bhagavad Gita 8.2

अधियज्ञः कथं कोऽत्र देहेऽस्मिन्मधुसूदन। प्रयाणकाले च कथं ज्ञेयोऽसि नियतात्मभिः।।8.2।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Who is the one called adhiyajna here in this body, Krishna, and how? And how are you known at the time of death by those who have mastered themselves?
