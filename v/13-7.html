Bhagavad Gita 13.6

इच्छा द्वेषः सुखं दुःखं सङ्घातश्चेतनाधृतिः।एतत्क्षेत्रं समासेन सविकारमुदाहृतम्।।13.7।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

desire, aversion, pleasure, pain, the aggregate (the body-mind collection), consciousness (chetana) and steadiness (dhriti) - this field has been described in brief, along with its changes.
