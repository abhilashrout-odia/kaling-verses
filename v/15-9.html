Bhagavad Gita 15.9

श्रोत्रं चक्षुः स्पर्शनं च रसनं घ्राणमेव च।अधिष्ठाय मनश्चायं विषयानुपसेवते।।15.9।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Presiding over the ear, eye, touch, taste and smell, and the mind too, he enjoys the objects of the senses.
