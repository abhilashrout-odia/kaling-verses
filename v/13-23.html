Bhagavad Gita 13.22

उपद्रष्टाऽनुमन्ता च भर्ता भोक्ता महेश्वरः।परमात्मेति चाप्युक्तो देहेऽस्मिन्पुरुषः परः।।13.23।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

The supreme person in this body is called the witness (upadrashta), the one who permits, the supporter, the experiencer, the great lord, and also the supreme self (paramatma).
