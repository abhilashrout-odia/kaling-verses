Bhagavad Gita 10.32

सर्गाणामादिरन्तश्च मध्यं चैवाहमर्जुन। अध्यात्मविद्या विद्यानां वादः प्रवदतामहम्।।10.32।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Of creations I am the beginning, end and middle, Arjuna. Among fields of knowledge I am knowledge of the Self; among disputants, reasoned debate.
