Bhagavad Gita 2.32

यदृच्छया चोपपन्नं स्वर्गद्वारमपावृतम्। सुखिनः क्षत्रियाः पार्थ लभन्ते युद्धमीदृशम्।।2.32।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Krishna says warriors are fortunate when such a battle comes to them unsought. He describes it to Arjuna as an open gate to heaven.
