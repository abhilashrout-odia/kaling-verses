Bhagavad Gita 18.53

अहङ्कारं बलं दर्पं कामं क्रोधं परिग्रहम्।विमुच्य निर्ममः शान्तो ब्रह्मभूयाय कल्पते।।18.53।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Having let go of egoism, force, arrogance, desire, anger and possessions, free of 'mine' and peaceful, one becomes fit for oneness with Brahman.
