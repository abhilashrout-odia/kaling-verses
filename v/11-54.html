Bhagavad Gita 11.54

भक्त्या त्वनन्यया शक्यमहमेवंविधोऽर्जुन। ज्ञातुं दृष्टुं च तत्त्वेन प्रवेष्टुं च परंतप।।11.54।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

But through undivided devotion, Arjuna, I can in this way be truly known, seen and entered, conqueror of foes.
