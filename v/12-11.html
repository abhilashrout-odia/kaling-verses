Bhagavad Gita 12.11

अथैतदप्यशक्तोऽसि कर्तुं मद्योगमाश्रितः।सर्वकर्मफलत्यागं ततः कुरु यतात्मवान्।।12.11।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

If you are unable even to do this, then, relying on my yoga and with a self-controlled mind, give up the rewards of all actions.
