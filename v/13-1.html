Bhagavad Gita Intro

अर्जुन उवाच प्रकृतिं पुरुषं चैव क्षेत्रं क्षेत्रज्ञमेव च। एतद्वेदितुमिच्छामि ज्ञानं ज्ञेयं च केशव।।13.1।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Arjuna said: Nature (prakriti) and the person (purusha), the field (kshetra) and the knower of the field (kshetrajna), knowledge and the object of knowledge - these I wish to know, Keshava.
