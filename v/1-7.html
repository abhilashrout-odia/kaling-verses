Bhagavad Gita 1.7

अस्माकं तु विशिष्टा ये तान्निबोध द्विजोत्तम। नायका मम सैन्यस्य संज्ञार्थं तान्ब्रवीमि ते।।1.7।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Now learn, best of the twice-born, who the leading commanders of my own army are. I will name them so that you know.
