Bhagavad Gita 8.14

अनन्यचेताः सततं यो मां स्मरति नित्यशः। तस्याहं सुलभः पार्थ नित्ययुक्तस्य योगिनः।।8.14।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

I am easily reached by the yogi who remembers me constantly with an undivided mind, Arjuna, and is always steady.
