Bhagavad Gita 14.17

सत्त्वात्सञ्जायते ज्ञानं रजसो लोभ एव च।प्रमादमोहौ तमसो भवतोऽज्ञानमेव च।।14.17।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

From sattva arises knowledge; from rajas greed; from tamas come carelessness, delusion and ignorance.
