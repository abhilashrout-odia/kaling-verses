Bhagavad Gita 6.20

यत्रोपरमते चित्तं निरुद्धं योगसेवया। यत्र चैवात्मनाऽऽत्मानं पश्यन्नात्मनि तुष्यति।।6.20।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

When the mind, held in check by the practice of yoga, becomes still, and one sees the self by the self and is content in the self,
