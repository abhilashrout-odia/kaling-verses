Bhagavad Gita 2.6

न चैतद्विद्मः कतरन्नो गरीयो यद्वा जयेम यदि वा नो जयेयुः। यानेव हत्वा न जिजीविषाम स्तेऽवस्थिताः प्रमुखे धार्तराष्ट्राः।।2.6।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Arjuna cannot tell whether winning or losing would be better. Dhritarashtra’s sons stand before him, and he would not want to live after killing them.
