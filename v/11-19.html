Bhagavad Gita 11.19

अनादिमध्यान्तमनन्तवीर्य मनन्तबाहुं शशिसूर्यनेत्रम्। पश्यामि त्वां दीप्तहुताशवक्त्रम् स्वतेजसा विश्वमिदं तपन्तम्।।11.19।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

I see you without beginning, middle or end, with infinite power and endless arms, the moon and sun as your eyes, blazing fire as your mouth, heating this world with your own radiance.

Hindi (AI drafted, not reviewed)
