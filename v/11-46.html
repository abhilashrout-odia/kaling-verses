Bhagavad Gita 11.46

किरीटिनं गदिनं चक्रहस्त मिच्छामि त्वां द्रष्टुमहं तथैव। तेनैव रूपेण चतुर्भुजेन सहस्रबाहो भव विश्वमूर्ते।।11.46।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

I wish to see you as before, crowned, bearing the mace and holding the discus. Thousand-armed one whose form is the world, take that four-armed form.
