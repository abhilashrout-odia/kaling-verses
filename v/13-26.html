Bhagavad Gita 13.25

अन्ये त्वेवमजानन्तः श्रुत्वाऽन्येभ्य उपासते।तेऽपि चातितरन्त्येव मृत्युं श्रुतिपरायणाः।।13.26।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Others, not knowing this, worship after hearing from others; those too, devoted to what they have heard (shruti), cross beyond death.
