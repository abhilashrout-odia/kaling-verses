Bhagavad Gita 4.34

तद्विद्धि प्रणिपातेन परिप्रश्नेन सेवया। उपदेक्ष्यन्ति ते ज्ञानं ज्ञानिनस्तत्त्वदर्शिनः।।4.34।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Learn the truth by approaching those who know, asking with humility, and serving them. Those wise people who have seen the truth will teach you knowledge.
