Bhagavad Gita 7.18

उदाराः सर्व एवैते ज्ञानी त्वात्मैव मे मतम्। आस्थितः स हि युक्तात्मा मामेवानुत्तमां गतिम्।।7.18।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

All of these are noble, but I regard the wise as my very self. Steady in mind, such a person takes me as the highest goal.
