Bhagavad Gita 17.7

आहारस्त्वपि सर्वस्य त्रिविधो भवति प्रियः।यज्ञस्तपस्तथा दानं तेषां भेदमिमं श्रृणु।।17.7।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Food too is dear to everyone in three kinds; so are offering (yajna), austerity and giving. Hear the difference among them.
