Bhagavad Gita 3.14

अन्नाद्भवन्ति भूतानि पर्जन्यादन्नसम्भवः। यज्ञाद्भवति पर्जन्यो यज्ञः कर्मसमुद्भवः।।3.14।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Beings live on food, food comes from rain, rain comes from offering (yajna), and offering comes from action.
