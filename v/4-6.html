Bhagavad Gita 4.6

अजोऽपि सन्नव्ययात्मा भूतानामीश्वरोऽपि सन्। प्रकृतिं स्वामधिष्ठाय संभवाम्यात्ममायया।।4.6।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Though unborn and unchanging, and the lord of all beings, I take my own nature and come into being through my own power.
