Bhagavad Gita 1.32

न काङ्क्षे विजयं कृष्ण न च राज्यं सुखानि च। किं नो राज्येन गोविन्द किं भोगैर्जीवितेन वा।।1.32।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Arjuna says he wants neither victory, nor a kingdom, nor pleasures. What use are a kingdom, enjoyments or even life itself to us?
