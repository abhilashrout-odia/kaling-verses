Bhagavad Gita 4.26

श्रोत्रादीनीन्द्रियाण्यन्ये संयमाग्निषु जुह्वति। शब्दादीन्विषयानन्य इन्द्रियाग्निषु जुह्वति।।4.26।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Some offer their hearing and other senses into the fires of self-restraint. Others offer sound and other sense objects into the fires of the senses.
