Bhagavad Gita 3.24

उत्सीदेयुरिमे लोका न कुर्यां कर्म चेदहम्। सङ्करस्य च कर्ता स्यामुपहन्यामिमाः प्रजाः।।3.24।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

If I did not act, these worlds would fall apart, and I would be the cause of confusion and the ruin of these beings.
