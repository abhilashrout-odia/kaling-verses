Bhagavad Gita 2.55

श्री भगवानुवाच प्रजहाति यदा कामान् सर्वान् पार्थ मनोगतान्। आत्मन्येवात्मना तुष्टः स्थितप्रज्ञस्तदोच्यते।।2.55।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Krishna says that when a person lets go of all desires arising in the mind and finds satisfaction in the Self alone, that person is called one of steady wisdom.
