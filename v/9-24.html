Bhagavad Gita 9.24

अहं हि सर्वयज्ञानां भोक्ता च प्रभुरेव च। न तु मामभिजानन्ति तत्त्वेनातश्च्यवन्ति ते।।9.24।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

I alone am the enjoyer and lord of all offerings (yajna). But they do not know me in truth, and therefore they fall.
