Bhagavad Gita 4.10

वीतरागभयक्रोधा मन्मया मामुपाश्रिताः। बहवो ज्ञानतपसा पूता मद्भावमागताः।।4.10।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

Free from attachment, fear and anger, absorbed in me and taking refuge in me, many people purified by the austerity of knowledge have reached my being.
