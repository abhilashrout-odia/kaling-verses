Bhagavad Gita 14.20

गुणानेतानतीत्य त्रीन्देही देहसमुद्भवान्।जन्ममृत्युजरादुःखैर्विमुक्तोऽमृतमश्नुते।।14.20।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

The embodied one, having gone beyond these three gunas that arise with the body, is freed from the pain of birth, death and old age, and reaches immortality.
