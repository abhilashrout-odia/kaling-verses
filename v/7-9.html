Bhagavad Gita 7.9

पुण्यो गन्धः पृथिव्यां च तेजश्चास्मि विभावसौ। जीवनं सर्वभूतेषु तपश्चास्मि तपस्विषु।।7.9।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

I am the pure fragrance of the earth and the brightness in fire. I am the life in all beings and the discipline in those who practice austerity.
