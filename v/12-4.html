Bhagavad Gita 12.4

संनियम्येन्द्रियग्रामं सर्वत्र समबुद्धयः।ते प्राप्नुवन्ति मामेव सर्वभूतहिते रताः।।12.4।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

having fully restrained the group of senses, with an even mind everywhere and devoted to the welfare of all beings, they too reach me alone.
