Bhagavad Gita 1.44

उत्सन्नकुलधर्माणां मनुष्याणां जनार्दन। नरकेऽनियतं वासो भवतीत्यनुशुश्रुम।।1.44।।

English (AI drafted, not reviewed)

We have heard, Krishna, that people whose family ways are destroyed must dwell for a long time in a place of suffering.
